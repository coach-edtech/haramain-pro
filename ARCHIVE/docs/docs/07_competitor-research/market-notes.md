## Purpose
Market context and observations about the Hajj/Umrah pilgrim market.

## Market Size
- **Indonesia Hajj pilgrims**: ~100,000/year (quota)
- **Indonesia Umrah pilgrims**: ~1,000,000+/year (growing)
- **Total addressable**: Indonesian Muslims planning pilgrimage

## Market Characteristics

### Buyer Journey
1. Register with travel agency (PPIU)
2. Embassy visa process
3. Travel to Saudi Arabia
4. Participate in pilgrimage
5. Return (become alumni)

### Key Pain Points
- **Safety**: No reliable emergency alert system
- **Coordination**: Large groups hard to manage via WhatsApp
- **Connectivity**: Poor WiFi/cellular in Saudi Arabia
- **Spiritual Guidance**: Generic content, not location-aware
- **Documentation**: No structured way to record spiritual journey

### Market Gaps
- No B2B platform for travel agencies
- No safety-focused pilgrim app
- No offline-first pilgrimage companion
- No structured group management

## Competitive Dynamics
- Fragmented market with many small players
- Agencies use WhatsApp for everything
- Limited tech adoption in pilgrimage space
- Price-sensitive market (but willing to pay for safety)
