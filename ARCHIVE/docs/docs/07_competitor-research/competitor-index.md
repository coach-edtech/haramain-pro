## Purpose
Index of known competitors in the Hajj/Umrah pilgrim app market.

## Direct Competitors

### 1. Assist Arabia
- **Type**: B2C Hajj/Umrah companion app
- **Strengths**: Well-established, large user base
- **Focus**: General pilgrimage guidance, not safety-focused

### 2. UmrahGuide
- **Type**: Mobile app + web platform
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive Dua library, route guidance
- **Focus**: Spiritual content, not group management

### 3. Various Hajj Apps
- Multiple individual apps with limited features
- No unified B2B solution for travel agencies
- Limited offline capability

### 4. Travel Agency Proprietary Apps
- Individual agency-branded apps
- Limited to agency's own pilgrims
- No cross-agency features

## Indirect Competitors

### 5. Google Maps / Maps.me
- Offline maps (competitor for offline feature)
- No pilgrimage-specific content

### 6. WhatsApp Groups
- How agencies currently coordinate groups
- No safety features, chaotic for large groups
