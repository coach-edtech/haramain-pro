## Purpose
North Star goals and measurable objectives for Haramain Pro.

## North Star
**Every pilgrim returns home safely, spiritually enriched, with their journey documented and shared.**

## Business Goals

### Q1/Q2 2026 — MVP Launch
- Launch PDPL-compliant consent flow
- Launch B2C Safety Pass with 7-day trial
- Onboard 3–5 pilot travel agencies (PPIU)
- Process first B2B volume license purchases
- Release panic alert with FCM + Twilio fallback

### Q3 2026 — Growth
- 1,000+ active Jamaah users
- 10+ agency partnerships
- Offline maps coverage for Mecca + Medina
- Virtual muthawif prayer trigger active

### Q4 2026 — Expansion
- Alumni broadcast feature live
- B2B admin dashboard
- Advanced group analytics
- Regional expansion (outside Java)

## Product Goals
- 90%+ panic alert delivery rate
- <5% subscription churn post-purchase
- Offline map load <2 seconds
- Jejak ibadah sync success >95%

## Compliance Goals
- 100% PDPL consent capture before data processing
- <24h data deletion request fulfillment
- Zero passport/biometric data in MVP
