## Purpose
Mapping of all stakeholders involved in Haramain Pro ecosystem.

## Primary Stakeholders

### Jamaah (Pilgrims)
- **Role**: End-users of the mobile app
- **Goals**: Safe pilgrimage, spiritual enrichment, group connectivity
- **Pain Points**: Connectivity issues in Mecca/Medina, language barriers, safety concerns

### Muthawif (Tour Guides)
- **Role**: Field operators guiding pilgrimage groups
- **Goals**: Group safety, coordinated logistics, spiritual guidance delivery
- **Pain Points**: Managing large groups, emergency response coordination

### Travel Agencies (PPIU)
- **Role**: B2B customers purchasing volume licenses
- **Goals**: Operational efficiency, passenger management, brand reputation
- **Pain Points**: Manual coordination, limited visibility into group status

### System Administrator
- **Role**: Platform oversight and support
- **Goals**: System health, user support, compliance
- **Tools**: Admin dashboard, DX tools
