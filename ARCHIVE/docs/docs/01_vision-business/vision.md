## Purpose
The overarching vision and mission of Haramain Pro.

## Vision Statement
To be the essential digital companion for every Muslim pilgrim — providing safety, spiritual guidance, and seamless group coordination from departure to return.

## Mission
- **Safety First**: Every pilgrim connected, protected, and reachable in emergencies
- **Spiritual Enrichment**: Contextual guidance that deepens the pilgrimage experience
- **Seamless Coordination**: Travel agencies and muthawif equipped with tools for flawless group management

## Core Values
1. **Accessibility** — Empowering pilgrims of all technical abilities
2. **Reliability** — Offline-capable, always-available critical features
3. **Privacy & Compliance** — PDPL-compliant data handling with explicit consent
4. **Community** — Connecting pilgrims, muthawif, and travel agencies in one ecosystem
